# 🛒 Retail Store Sales Analysis

### 📊 Power BI Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics Project

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze retail store performance, sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, payment methods, and returns.

This project transforms retail transaction data into **actionable business insights** that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Store Sales Analysis** project provides a comprehensive view of retail business performance across sales, stores, products, customers, profitability, and returns.

The dashboard consists of **4 interactive pages**, each designed for a specific analytical purpose:

1. 🏠 **Executive Overview**
2. 🏪 **Store & Product Analysis**
3. 👥 **Customer Analysis**
4. 🎯 **Management Action Center**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Track monthly sales trends
- Compare performance across different stores
- Identify top and bottom-performing products
- Analyze category-wise sales and profitability
- Understand customer segments and demographics
- Analyze payment method preferences
- Monitor product and category return rates
- Identify key business performance drivers
- Generate actionable management recommendations

# 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- 💰 Total sales reached approximately **17.00M**.
- 💵 Total profit was approximately **7.15M**.
- 📈 Overall profit margin was **42.08%**.
- 🏪 **Chennai Central** recorded the highest store-level sales among the displayed top-performing stores.
- 👟 **Running Shoes** was the leading product by sales.
- 👗 **Fashion** generated the highest category sales.
- 👥 The **Regular customer segment** contributed the largest share of customer sales.
- 💳 **UPI** represented the largest share among the displayed payment methods.
- 🔄 Overall return rate was **5.75%**, with **460 total returns**.
- 👤 Customer analysis highlights differences in purchasing behavior across segments and age groups.

# 🎯 Management Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following areas can be considered for business improvement:

### 1. 🏪 Store Performance
Monitor high-performing stores and identify the operational factors contributing to their sales and profitability.

### 2. 📦 Product Strategy
Maintain availability of high-demand products while reviewing low-performing products for pricing, promotion, or inventory decisions.

### 3. 🛍️ Category Management
Focus on high-contribution categories while improving the profitability of lower-margin categories.

### 4. 👥 Customer Engagement
Develop targeted strategies for Regular, New, and Premium customer segments.

### 5. 💳 Payment Optimization
Promote convenient digital payment options while monitoring payment-method trends.

### 6. 🔄 Return Reduction
Investigate categories with higher return rates and identify potential causes such as product quality, customer expectations, or product information.

### 7. 📈 Profitability Monitoring
Track profit margin alongside sales rather than evaluating store or product performance using sales alone.
